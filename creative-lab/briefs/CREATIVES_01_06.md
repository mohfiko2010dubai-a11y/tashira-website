# Six initial TASHIRA creative briefs

All final assets use the unchanged TASHIRA logo and deterministic text overlays. Every format includes the disclosure: **Private travel/tourism service provider. Approval remains subject to relevant UAE authorities.**

## Creative 01 — GCC Residents static

- Audience/format: GCC residents; 1:1 Instagram/Facebook.
- Objective: introduce the GCC resident path.
- Hook A: **GCC Resident? Plan Your UAE Visit**
- Hook B: **Your UAE Visa Application, Online**
- Headline: **UAE Visa Support for GCC Residents**
- Caption: Choose the relevant option, complete your details, upload documents and apply online with TASHIRA.
- CTA: **Apply Online**
- Art direction: confident GCC resident traveler at an airport-window or Dubai arrival setting; navy editorial panel and gold CTA; no government marks.
