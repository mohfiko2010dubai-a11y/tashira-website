# Production storyboards

## Shared production rules

- Canvas: 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC.
- Safe area: 108 px sides, 230 px top/bottom.
- Generated media contains no text, logos, visa documents, UI, or government insignia.
- FFmpeg renders all headlines, subtitles, TASHIRA logo, CTA, and disclosure.
- Transitions: 6–10 frame dissolves or clean match cuts; avoid novelty effects.
- Audio: warm modern instrumental at -22 LUFS under narration; commercial-safe license required.
