# TASHIRA creative brand guide

Source of truth: current TASHIRA repository and production website language as reviewed on 22 August 2026.

## Identity

- Brand: **TASHIRA**
- Legal name when required: **TASHIRA E-Visa & Tourism LLC-FZ**
- Role: private travel/tourism and visa-application support provider.
- Required disclosure: **TASHIRA is a private travel/tourism service provider. Visa approval and timing remain subject to the relevant UAE authorities.**

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight | `#1A2332` | Headline, premium dark surfaces |
| Gold | `#C9A04C` | CTA and key accent |
| Light gold | `#DDBB7A` | Gradient/highlight |
| Ivory | `#FAFAF7` | Warm background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and contrast |

Use the repository logo unchanged. Use clean sans-serif typography, generous whitespace, rounded cards, subtle borders and shadows, and high-contrast mobile-first text. Generated imagery should be premium, natural, believable, and recognizably travel-focused without government insignia.

## Approved messages

- Apply for a UAE visa online.
- Choose a visa type, enter details, upload documents, pay securely, and track the application.
- Secure online payment and application tracking are available.
- TASHIRA supports the application process; the relevant authorities decide approval.

## Prohibited messages

Never use: guaranteed visa, 100% approval, guaranteed processing time, official UAE Government website, government-approved visa issuer, fake urgency, fabricated ratings, fabricated customer totals, or an unsupported price.
