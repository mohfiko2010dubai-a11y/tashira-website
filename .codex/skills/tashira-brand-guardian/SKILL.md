# TASHIRA Brand Guardian

Read [references/brand-system.md](references/brand-system.md) before producing TASHIRA-facing creative.

Use the current website and repository as the factual source. Preserve the approved company name, palette, logo proportions, concise mobile-first hierarchy, and private-provider disclosure. Prefer deterministic typography and logo overlays over AI-rendered text or logos.

Never claim or imply guaranteed approval, guaranteed processing time, government affiliation, official-government status, or authority to issue a visa. Do not invent prices, ratings, customer counts, testimonials, awards, processing times, or eligibility rules.

Every visa creative must remain compatible with this disclosure: TASHIRA is a private travel/tourism service provider; visa decisions remain subject to the relevant UAE authorities. Make the disclosure legible for the format rather than hiding it.

Before approval, check logo integrity, palette, type hierarchy, spacing, image realism, CTA clarity, factual support, disclosure visibility, and mobile readability.
