# TASHIRA brand system

- Approved name: **TASHIRA**. Legal name where needed: **TASHIRA E-Visa & Tourism LLC-FZ**.
- Position: private UAE visa-application support and travel/tourism service provider.
- Primary colors: midnight navy `#1A2332`, TASHIRA gold `#C9A04C`, light gold `#DDBB7A`, warm ivory `#FAFAF7`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- Type: clean geometric sans-serif; bold concise headline, short supporting copy, high contrast. Use the existing site font stack when available.
- Logo: use the repository SVG/PNG unchanged. Keep clear space of at least the height of the T mark; no recoloring, warping, regeneration, or placement over busy detail.
- Layout: premium whitespace, centered grids, subtle borders/shadows, rounded corners, gold CTA on navy/ivory.
- Image direction: premium natural travel photography, believable people, recognizable but non-governmental UAE context, warm daylight, no fake interfaces or documents.
- CTA vocabulary: `Apply Online`, `Start Application`, `Track Application`, `View Visa Options`.
- Voice: clear, reassuring, concise, practical, transparent. Avoid hype and urgency pressure.
- Required meaning: approval and timing are subject to relevant UAE authorities; TASHIRA supports the application process and is not a government authority.
- Prohibited: `Guaranteed visa`, `100% approval`, `Government-approved visa issuer`, `Official UAE Government website`, guaranteed timing, fabricated scarcity, fake reviews or statistics.
