# TASHIRA Post-Launch Roadmap

These items are intentionally outside V1 launch closure unless new evidence makes one release-critical:

- AI CFO and optional dashboard enhancements.
- Advanced funnel analytics beyond the verified-payment conversion.
- React Router major-version migration.
- XLSX library replacement and export redesign.
- Major dependency migrations and remaining non-Critical audit remediation.
- Further client/server bundle optimization.
- Alternate Payment Link flows.
- Optional visual redesign.
- Expanded document/status email automation and Admin retry UI.
- Long-term transactional outbox beyond provider and webhook idempotency.
- Final legal retention, deletion, consent, and refund-policy implementation after owner/legal decisions.
